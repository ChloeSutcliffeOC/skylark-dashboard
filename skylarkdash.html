Debrief Note ✓

    

      

        After you land. Anything that happened, anything that surprised you, anything the next person should know.
        Short is fine — “nothing to report” is a valid answer, and a near miss written down badly beats one not written down at all.
      

      
🎤 Use the keyboard's microphone key on this iPad. Tap the note, then tap the mic key at the bottom right of the on-screen keyboard and speak. In-page dictation cannot get the microphone in a Home Screen app.

Emergency Procedures ✓

    

    


      

        

          

          Pilot Incapacitation
          "Terminate mission"
        

        

          
    
            
  1. 1Increase altitude if required
    2. 
            
  2. 2Activate RTH failsafe
    3. 
            
  3. 3Immediately clear the area of people
    4. 
            
  4. 4UAS will automatically land and shut motors off
    5. 
            
  5. 5Seek medical assistance
    6. 
          

          
        

      


      

        

          

          Air Incursion
          "Air incursion"
        

        

          
    
            
  1. 1Assess heading, speed and altitude of the incursion
    2. 
            
  2. 2Assess heading, speed and altitude of the UA
    3. 
            
  3. 3Acquire orientation of the UA
    4. 
            
  4. 4See and avoid — change altitude and move UA at 90° to the hazard's flight path
    5. 
          

          
        

      


      

        

          

          Ground Incursion
          "Cordon breach"
        

        

          
    
            
  1. 1Move UAS to a holding area at a safe and legal distance from the uninvolved person(s)
    2. 
            
  2. 2Clear area of uninvolved person(s), if possible
    3. 
            
  3. 3Resume mission if area is cleared — or land at alternative location if persons cannot be cleared
    4. 
          

          
        

      


      

        

          

          Fire
          "Fire, fire, fire"
        

        

          
    
            
  1. 1Identify a non-flammable landing area
    2. 
            
  2. 2Immediately clear the area of people
    3. 
            
  3. 3Land as soon as possible
    4. 
            
  4. 4Put out fire, if trained, equipped and safe to do so
    5. 
            
  5. 5Call emergency services if required
    6. 
          

          
        

      


      

        

          

          Flyaway
          "Flyaway"
        

        

          
    
            
  1. 1Note time and heading
    2. 
            
  2. 2Calculate remaining flight time and plot potential crash site
    3. 
            
  3. 3Leave command unit on and monitor telemetry
    4. 
            
  4. 4Inform appropriate ATC and/or airspace managers in direction of flyaway
    5. 
            
  5. 5Inform police
    6. 
            
  6. 6Remain at AOO until UAS returns via RTH or runs out of fuel
    7. 
            
  7. 7Find crash site using command unit map or obtain lat/lon from digital flight log
    8. 
          

          
        

      


      

        

          

          Abnormal Weather — Visibility
          "Bad vis"
        

        

          
    
            
  1. 1Move UA closer to RP to ensure clear visibility
    2. 
            
  2. 2Terminate flight if there is a risk of VLOS not being maintained
    3. 
          

          
        

      


      

        

          

          Abnormal Weather — Wind
          "Wind"
        

        

          
    
            
  1. 1Pause and hover (MR) or enter loiter mode (FW)
    2. 
            
  2. 2Note wind direction and strength
    3. 
            
  3. 3Try to regain control
    4. 
            
  4. 4If control not regained, engage RTH
    5. 
            
  5. 5Identify landing site and immediately clear the area of people
    6. 
            
  6. 6UAS will automatically land and shut motors off
    7.

Voice Debrief ✓

    

    

      
🎤 Use the keyboard’s microphone key on this iPad. Tap a field, then tap the mic key at the bottom right of the on-screen keyboard and speak. Tap it again to finish, then Sort into rows as usual. In-page dictation cannot get the microphone in a Home Screen app.

      
⚠ Speech to text isn’t available in this browser. On iPad or iPhone use Safari — Chrome and Edge on iOS cannot do it. Otherwise use Chrome on a laptop, or type straight into the fields.

      

        
🎤 Dictate the whole debrief

        
Talk through the debrief as you normally would, naming each area as you reach it — weather, timings, objectives, safe flight, cordon, airspace, comms, phases of the operation, what went well, what could be improved, questions. Skylark drops each part into the matching row and sets the Yes/No answers where it can. Before sorting, Skylark checks the transcript against OC vocabulary and shows you every word it changed. Always read the rows back before saving. Voice needs a data connection — with no signal on site, type straight into the rows.
